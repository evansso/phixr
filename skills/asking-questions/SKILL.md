---
name: asking-questions
description: Load before calling askUserQuestionnaire.
---
- Before asking, list everything you need to know to choose your next action. Ask all unknowns in one questionnaire.
- Questions that depend on an earlier answer wait. Everything independent goes together.
- Ask only what the user can know and your tools cannot find out. Each question must change your next action.
- Max 4 questions. Most diagnostic first. If you need more, keep the 4 that change your action most.
- Prefer choices over free text. Turn time questions into ranges: today, this week, longer ago. Add allowOther when your options may miss. Include an "I haven't checked" option when the user may not know.
- Cover what is missing from: environment (OS, version, hardware), scope (always or sometimes, one network or all, one device or all), timing (when it started, what changed), and what they already tried.
- Mark a question required only if you cannot proceed without it.
- Never ask for something already stated earlier.
